Selected Projects

Fibi — Voice-First Personal Finance & Inventory App

React Native (Expo) - TypeScript - SQLite - Node.js - LLM
  • Built a mobile app that turns a single spoken or typed sentence (“bought headphones for 3700 lira”) into both an expense and an inventory record. Input is first handled by an on-device rule-based parser; ambiguous sentences are routed to LLMs through a multi-layer fallback chain, with amount and date always verified in code.
  • Includes installment, subscription and monthly cash-flow planning, investment tracking with live prices (stocks, gold, FX, crypto), and expense suggestions from bank SMS. Local-first architecture keeps all data on-device in SQLite, with JSON/CSV backup and restore.
